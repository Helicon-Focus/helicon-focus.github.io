Helicon Focus

Helicon Focus blends the sharpest areas from multiple partially focused photographs into a single, perfectly focused image. Designed for macro, micro, and hyperfocal landscape photography, it solves shallow depth-of-field challenges with precision and efficiency.

Key Advantages

Unlimited Depth of Field

Combine stacks of images to achieve total sharpness across your subject, from foreground to background. Ideal for macro and micro work where focus limits traditional shots.

Intelligent Alignment

Automatically aligns images, compensating for shifts and scale changes common in macro photography or stereomicroscopy. Ensures seamless stacking without manual adjustments.

Retouching Brush

Clone details directly from source images onto the final result. Non-destructive editing integrated into the workflow—no external software needed.

Advanced Processing

64-bit engine leverages full RAM for high-resolution stacks. Supports RAW, 16-bit depth, and unlimited image counts with Lanczos and Sinc256 interpolators for maximum detail preservation.

Versatile Output

Export to TIFF, JPEG, PSD, PNG, or even 3D models and animated stacks. Dust removal, brightness matching, and 2D micropanorama built-in.

How It Works

Step 1: Capture Stack

Shoot a series of images while incrementally adjusting focus across your subject. Use a rail or automated system for consistency.

Step 2: Load and Align

Import your stack into Helicon Focus. The software automatically detects and aligns images, handling scale and position variations.

Step 3: Render Focus

Select a rendering method and process. Advanced algorithms blend in-focus areas while preserving colors and details in 16-bit depth.

Step 4: Refine and Export

Use the retouching brush for fixes, remove dust, and export your fully focused image or 3D model.
